=========================================================

Colosseum is thrilled to announce the results of the [Solana Cypherpunk Hackathon](https://colosseum.com/cypherpunk?ref=blog.colosseum.com), a global online competition focused on bringing the next wave of high-impact products into the Solana ecosystem. Cypherpunk was the 12th Solana Foundation hackathon and the 4th run by Colosseum. The competition brought together founders and developers from 150+ countries to build products spanning Infrastructure, Consumer, DeFi, Stablecoins, RWAs, and a long-tail of Undefined Applications.

[More than 9,000 participants teamed up to submit 1,576 final projects](https://arena.colosseum.org/projects/explore?seed=2299a77f2da75968&hackathonId=4&ref=blog.colosseum.com), making Cypherpunk the largest crypto hackathon to date. Phantom, Arcium, Forward Industries, Raydium, Reflect, and Triton joined the Solana Foundation as sponsors for the hackathon, offering a platform for crypto founders to jumpstart their journey.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a7/57/a7579d3a-5c65-4b08-bb11-1ef78f2e0172/content/images/2025/12/Screenshot-2025-12-03-at-7.27.36---PM.png)

"Solana Hackathons demonstrate the rapid growth of both developer talent and innovation within the ecosystem," said Dan Albert, Executive Director of the Solana Foundation. "The ideas and founders emerging from the Solana Cypherpunk Hackathon are driving real momentum, and the next wave of products will strengthen the ecosystem."

Without further ado, here are the winners…

### **Grand Champion**

[Unruggable](https://arena.colosseum.org/projects/explore/unruggable-3?previous=L3Byb2plY3RzL2V4cGxvcmU_c2VlZD0yMjk5YTc3ZjJkYTc1OTY4JmhhY2thdGhvbklkPTUmc2VhcmNoPXVucnVnZ2FibGU&ref=blog.colosseum.com), a new hardware wallet and companion app built for Solana, will receive $30,000 USDC.

The first prize goes to [Capitola](https://arena.colosseum.org/projects/explore/capitola?previous=L3Byb2plY3RzL2V4cGxvcmU_c2VlZD0xNzc5MmUyMTgwNzA0ZTAzJnNlYXJjaD1jYXBpdG9sYQ&ref=blog.colosseum.com), a prediction markets meta-aggregator, with an award of $25,000 in USDC.

* Second: [Superfan](https://arena.colosseum.org/projects/explore/superfan?ref=blog.colosseum.com), a new a meta-record label for artists and their fans.

* Fourth: [Toaster.trade](https://arena.colosseum.org/projects/explore/toaster.trade?ref=blog.colosseum.com), a TikTok-style socialfi platform powered by Hyperliquid and Solana
