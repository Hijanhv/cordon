=======================================================

Colosseum is thrilled to announce the results of the [Solana Breakout Hackathon](https://www.colosseum.com/breakout?ref=blog.colosseum.com), a global online competition focused on bringing the next wave of high-impact projects into the Solana ecosystem. Breakout was the 11th Solana Foundation hackathon and the 3rd run by Colosseum. The competition brought together founders and developers from 140+ countries to build products spanning Infrastructure, Gaming, DePIN, Stablecoins, DeFi, AI, and a long-tail of Consumer Applications.

[More than 10,000 participants teamed up to submit 1,412 final projects](https://arena.colosseum.org/projects/explore?seed=2299a77f2da75968&hackathonId=4&ref=blog.colosseum.com) to the judges, making Breakout the largest crypto hackathon to date. Pump.fun, Wormhole, MagicBlock, Render, Perena, Walrus, Metaplex, and Replit joined the Solana Foundation as sponsors for the hackathon, offering a platform for crypto founders to jumpstart their journey.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a7/57/a7579d3a-5c65-4b08-bb11-1ef78f2e0172/content/images/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-05-31-at-5.31.12---PM.png)

"Solana Hackathons demonstrate the rapid growth of both developer talent and innovation within the ecosystem," said Dan Albert, Executive Director of the Solana Foundation. "The ideas and founders emerging from the Solana Breakout Hackathon are driving real momentum, and the next wave of products will strengthen the ecosystem."

Without further ado, here are the winners…

### **Grand Champion**

[TapeDrive](https://arena.colosseum.org/projects/explore/tapedrive?ref=blog.colosseum.com), a scalable decentralized storage network that rewards you for storing data received $50,000 USDC along with passes to attend Breakpoint 2025 in Abu Dhabi, UAE.
